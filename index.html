About the gym

Built for every body, every goal.

FitZone Gym is a modern, air-conditioned training facility in Vashi, Navi Mumbai. Whether you are starting out or chasing a competition, our floor is designed to keep you safe, motivated and progressing.

Our mission

To make professional-quality coaching and a clean, welcoming gym accessible to everyone in Navi Mumbai — with honest advice, no shortcuts and no pressure.

Facilities

  • Free-weight & machine zone
  • Cardio deck with 20+ machines
  • Functional training area
  • Separate yoga & Zumba studio
  • Locker rooms with showers
  • Certified trainers on floor
  • Fresh water & protein bar
  • Air-conditioned workout space
